## Prompt Testing in Generative AI

### Prompt 1
**Input:** What is Artificial Intelligence?  
**Output:** Artificial Intelligence is the ability of machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

### Prompt 2
**Input:** Explain neural networks in simple words.  
**Output:** Neural networks are computing systems inspired by the human brain that learn patterns from data.

### Prompt 3
**Input:** What is Generative AI?  
**Output:** Generative AI refers to models that can create new content such as text, images, or code.

### Observation
Different prompts lead to different generated responses. The clarity and specificity of the prompt directly affect the quality of the generated output.
