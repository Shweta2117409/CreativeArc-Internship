-TASK 3: OUTLIERS – THINK BEFORE TOUCHING

For outlier analysis, numeric columns were first identified and examined
using the IQR method. Among these, the Rating column was selected for
decision-making.

Although outliers were detected in the Rating column, they were
intentionally kept rather than removed or capped. Extremely high or low
ratings often represent genuine user experiences, such as highly
successful apps with exceptional feedback or poorly performing apps
with negative reviews. Treating these values as errors would remove
important real-world signals from the data.

Therefore, restricting outlier handling to a single, carefully chosen
column helps balance data robustness with real-world realism.

-TASK 5: EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS INSIGHTS

The exploratory analysis reveals that most applications on the Play Store
receive relatively high ratings, typically between 4.0 and 4.5, indicating
generally positive user feedback across the platform. Extremely low-rated
apps are uncommon.

Popularity, measured through install counts, does not directly guarantee
higher ratings. While highly installed apps often maintain stable ratings,
newer or less popular apps show greater variability in user feedback.

Pricing also does not appear to be a strong determinant of user ratings.
Most apps are free, and paid apps do not consistently outperform free apps
in terms of ratings. This implies that app quality and user satisfaction
are driven more by functionality and usability rather than cost.
